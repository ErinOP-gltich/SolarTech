01 Who we are

Green Sun Technologies is a Guwahati-born engineering company. We design, manufacture, sell and service solar and LED lighting — from a 9-watt village-lane light to a 12-metre high mast — and stand behind every one of them.

A — Design

We size it

Every road is different. We match wattage, battery and panel to your site, your hours and your weather — not to a brochure.

B — Manufacture

We build it

Aluminium housings, OSRAM LEDs at 155 lm/W, LiFePO₄ packs and IP66 sealing — assembled by a registered manufacturing MSME.

C — Install & service

We stay on it

Our team is a phone call away on NH-37. Installation, maintenance, battery swaps and remote monitoring when you need it.
